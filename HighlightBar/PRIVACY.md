# Highlight Bar — Privacy Policy

Highlight Bar does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app runs entirely on your Mac. It has no account system, no analytics, and
no tracking. Your settings are stored only on your own device, in your user
preferences. Highlight Bar requests no special system permissions to run its
core features.

The Mac App Store version makes no network connections. (The separate
direct-download version checks for updates only when you choose "Check for
Updates" — no personal data is sent in that check.)

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or
delete.
