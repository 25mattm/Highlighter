## Description (max 4000)
Highlight Bar is a gentle reading guide that floats over everything on your screen, so your eyes never lose the line.

A soft, cursor-following bar highlights the line you're reading. Dim everything else with a spotlight, or wash the whole screen in a calming tint. It sits on top of any app — your browser, PDFs, documents, code — and clicks pass straight through, so it never gets in your way.

Built first as an accessibility tool, Highlight Bar is designed for readers who find long text hard to track: people with dyslexia, ADHD, low vision, or visual stress (Irlen) — and anyone facing a long document at the end of a long day.

FEATURES
• Reading bar that follows your cursor or scroll, or locks in place
• Spotlight mode — dim the page and shine a light on the current line
• Screen tint — a full-screen color wash for visual-stress relief
• Any color you like — pick the exact bar or tint that's easiest on your eyes
• Adjustable size, thickness, and opacity
• Ruler or thin-line shapes, horizontal or vertical
• Profiles for different reading needs
• Auto-enable only in the apps you choose
• Show or hide instantly with a keyboard shortcut

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Highlight Bar runs entirely on your Mac. It collects no data, needs no account, and asks for no special permissions. Nothing ever leaves your device.
